## How Betacms organizes content

- **Projects** organize your work. Projects can have a description, cover image, folder, and archived state.
- **Collections** belong to a project and define a reusable content structure, such as a blog or product catalog.
- **Fields** describe the data each collection stores. Fields have a type, key, name, required setting, and optional description.
- **Items** are the individual records in a collection. Each item has a slug, an order, a draft or live status, and field values.
