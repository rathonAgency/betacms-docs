## Before you begin

Open the [Betacms dashboard](https://betacms.rathon.agency). Decide what kind of content you want to publish, such as blog posts, products, or case studies.

## Create your content

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Create a project for the site or content area where this content belongs. Add a title and, if useful, a description, cover image, or folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a collection">
    Add a collection to the project. Give it a clear name that describes the type of content it will contain, such as Blog posts or Authors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fields">
    Define the information each item needs. For a blog collection, you might use a title, rich-text body, image, publication date, and category. Set fields as required when every item needs a value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an item">
    Add an item to the collection and fill in its field values. Betacms assigns each item a slug for its URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the item">
    Keep work in draft while it is being prepared. Set its status to live when it is ready to appear on your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect content to your site

Use the collection to power a dynamic page on your site. When you update and publish an item in Betacms, the content can flow through to the page connected to that collection.

For ideas on structuring your data, see [Collections and the content model](/features/collections-guide). For item states and ordering, see [Manage items](/features/items-guide).
